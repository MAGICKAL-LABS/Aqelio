Install
1. Make a backup of your current three files.
2. Replace your project's index.html, app.js, and style.css with these versions. Keep the files together in the same folder.
3. Open Aqelio as usual. Premium tools are locked by default. The free task manager, calendar, categories, and focus features are unchanged.

Preview unlocked Premium locally (development only)
1. Open Aqelio in your browser.
2. Open Developer Tools > Console.
3. Run: localStorage.setItem("aqelio_premium", "true"); location.reload();
4. To lock it again: localStorage.removeItem("aqelio_premium"); location.reload();

Important
This is a paywall UI and client-side prototype, not a live payment system. The $4.99/month price is illustrative and no payment is processed. The local flag can be changed by users, so do not sell access until purchases are verified through a trusted payment provider/backend.
